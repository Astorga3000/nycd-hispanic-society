New York Correction Department Hispanic Society

Be connected. Be informed. Be heard. A fraternal organization for members of Hispanic descent in the New York City Department of Correction.

Become a member See events

Mission statement

The Hispanic Society’s goal is to promote and develop a friendly, fraternal spirit among all members of Hispanic descent in the Correction Department.

We create a strong forum for resolving bias, unethical or discriminatory treatment within the Department.

We also serve as a bridge to build a more harmonious relationship between the Department and the Hispanic community.

Become a member today

Annual dues are $30

Sign up on ESS (Employee Self Service)

  1. Log into your ESS account.
  2. Click “Pay and tax information.”
  3. Open the “Deduction information” dropdown folder.
  4. Click “DCNY Fraternal Org Dues.”
  5. Click “Enroll” to confirm your membership with the DOC Hispanic Society.

Then complete the application form.
